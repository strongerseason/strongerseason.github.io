Meet your trainer

Hi, I'm Kate.

I believe getting stronger isn't just about what happens in the gym. It's about creating the strength, mobility, and confidence to fully enjoy the life you want to live.

I've been active since my twenties, but my own fitness journey has evolved through different seasons of life. I went from living for spin classes, hikes, barre, and Pilates to discovering how transformative strength training could be.

Today, I'm passionate about helping women build the kind of strength that carries into everyday life: traveling with confidence, playing better golf, getting outside, keeping up with the people they love, and saying yes to new experiences.

As a personal trainer and mobility coach, I work with women who are entering a new season of life and want to feel strong, capable, and comfortable in their bodies. Whether you're rebuilding after having children, starting over after a major life change, rediscovering activities you once loved, or simply deciding it's time to prioritize yourself, I'm here to help you focus on you.

Movement and strength strategies for women who want to stay active wherever life takes them.
Philosophy

Strength, every season.

I help women build strength, mobility, stability, and confidence so they can move through life's transitions and fully enjoy the chapter they're in. Every program comes back to four things.

Strength

Progressive strength training to build muscle, bone strength, power, and confidence.

Movement

Mobility, stability, balance, and functional movement so you can move well for life.

Play

Training for the activities that make life fun — golf, hiking, travel, skiing, pickleball, and more.

Live

Using fitness to support a bigger life, rather than making fitness the entire goal.
